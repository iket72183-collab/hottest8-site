01 / Methodology

How we rank

Summed follows, not unique people
Combined = public X follows + public Instagram follows on the listed accounts. One person may be counted on both platforms. This is not a measure of attractiveness, engagement, authenticity or revenue.
A tracked set, not everyone
Our scope is adult women in adult entertainment, including retired creators. The Top 8 is drawn only from the tracked set. Inclusion and adult status require maintainer review; follower counts do not establish either.
Unknown is not zero
Combined needs both counts. A missing or unverified Instagram count excludes that creator from Combined and Instagram views; an available X count can still rank in the X view. Numeric zero is a valid observation.
Equal counts share a rank
Competition ranking is used: 1, 2, 2, 4. Tied entries are ordered by X handle, case-insensitively. The chart has eight slots; a tie at the boundary may extend beyond those slots. Exact counts are visible beneath the rounded total.
Dates belong to each platform
X and Instagram are not necessarily checked together. Each card shows the recorded platform observation date; carried-forward Instagram counts retain their old date. A warning appears when the overall snapshot is over three days old. A successful page load is not a fresh measurement.
Identity needs evidence
Only maintainer-reviewed official accounts are listed. Excluding an alternate or fan account from ranking does not make it fake. Stable platform IDs should be recorded on refresh; handle changes and identity conflicts require review before histories are joined.
SFW presentation
Only the existing, fixed X-avatar URLs recorded in the reviewed allowlist can load. Unapproved or failed images fall back to initials. No new photos are scraped or rehosted. A future avatar change needs SFW and permission review. External creator links may contain adult material.
Refresh responsibility
Grok Bot maintains the recorded snapshots. The site targets daily X refreshes; Instagram may be older. See the source note below and the public JSON for the actual dates. No refresh credentials or scheduler run in your browser.

    03 / Movement

    Growth with context

    Rising 8 uses a separate emerging-creator pool, excluding the current Top 8. A creator needs at least 10,000 follows at the start and a verified combined total no greater than 1 million at the end. If Instagram is unknown, the cap cannot be confirmed, so the entry is held out.

    Growth = end minus start. Percentage growth = growth ÷ start × 100. We compare the latest observation to the most recent observation at or before seven days earlier, or the earliest available observation for a shorter history. The actual hours and both timestamps are always shown. Sparse data is never relabeled as an exact seven-day measurement.

    Stable X IDs join renamed accounts. A reused handle with a different ID is not the same account. Legacy records without IDs require unchanged names and handles, and cannot independently prove identity. Instagram growth requires the same account handle/ID, verified counts and a later check date; carried-forward values are not fresh growth. New Instagram coverage is not follower growth.

    Positive growth is ordered by unrounded percentage, then absolute gain; exact metric ties share a rank and use handle order. Zero and negative growth remain in the JSON evidence but not in the Rising 8. Milestones compare consecutive comparable observations; closest battles compare adjacent ranks, with their platform labeled.

        05 / Your information

        Privacy & email choices

        Browsing and age confirmation
        The 18+ site-entry choice is stored in your browser as h8age. It is a self-declaration, not identity verification. Clearing site storage removes it. The hosting provider receives ordinary request information such as your IP address.
        Newsletter purpose and consent
        The existing EmailOctopus form is for the weekly Top 8 rankings and The Industry Dispatch, a digest of public industry news. It is not a separate FakeCheck early-access list. You choose whether to load the form; loading alone does not subscribe you. EmailOctopus processes submitted details, including your email address and its form’s age-confirmation field, and may process technical and delivery data under its own policy.
        Unsubscribe and retention
        Use the unsubscribe link in newsletter emails to stop marketing, or contact hello@hottest8.com for help or deletion requests. Unsubscribing does not necessarily erase every record immediately: suppression, consent and provider records may be retained. A specific deletion period and an end-to-end unsubscribe test have not been verified in this site’s code audit.
        Analytics
        Production uses GoatCounter for page-view analytics after the age gate. GoatCounter describes its service as cookie-free; request metadata is still processed. See GoatCounter’s privacy documentation. This site does not count an attempted form submission as a confirmed subscription.
        Third-party resources
        Approved avatar URLs load directly from X’s image servers. The newsletter form loads from EmailOctopus only after you request it, and may load reCAPTCHA or other provider anti-abuse resources. Those providers receive ordinary request metadata. Typography uses local system fonts. No ad network scripts are included.
        Provider controls
        Opt-in settings, signup rate limits, CAPTCHA enforcement, email tracking, unsubscribe delivery and retention are controlled in the provider account. We do not claim those controls have been independently tested here. EmailOctopus privacy policy.

        Bring your own reader.

        Read hottest8.com/rankings.json and hottest8.com/insights.json. Summarize the tracked Top 8 and Rising 8, quoting the actual observation dates. Do not invent weekly growth when less than a week of comparable history is available.
