        How we rank

        • Combined score = public X followers + public Instagram followers on each creator's official account. These are follows, not unique people, and not a measure of attractiveness.
        • Coverage: women in adult entertainment (current or retired performers/creators) with official X and Instagram accounts. We currently track 10; the list is not a complete census, so the Top 8 is the top 8 of our tracked set.
        • Sources: X counts via X API, checked Oct 21, 2026 ~9:10 AM CT. Instagram counts via Instagram API, checked Oct 20, 2026 ~8:30 PM CT (Muse research). Missing data is shown as unknown, never zero; if a refresh fails we keep the last verified snapshot and its date.
        • Official accounts only — verified, or linked from the creator's own profiles. Fan and backup accounts are excluded.
        • Rising 8 = creators ranked by percentage follower growth between dated daily snapshots (absolute gain shown too). Until 7 days of history exist, the window is labeled with its real start (e.g. “since Oct 20”); after that it is 7-day growth. A creator needs two real data points to qualify. Instagram growth counts only when Instagram was actually re-checked; otherwise growth is X only and labeled so.
        • Milestones and Closest Battles are computed from the same snapshots: round-million crossings, and the smallest gaps between neighbouring ranks.
        • Updated daily from public data. Profile pictures are the creator's own X avatar, linked by a fixed URL we reviewed for SFW; if an avatar can't be verified as SFW we show initials instead. We host no images.
        • Retired creators stay listed but are badged.
